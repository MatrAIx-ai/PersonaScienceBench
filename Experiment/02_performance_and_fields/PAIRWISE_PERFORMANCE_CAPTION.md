# Selected-persona pairwise performance figure

Panel A reports available native scores normalized to 0–100 with pointwise 95% study-cluster bootstrap intervals. Panel B reports row-minus-column score changes on compatible question–trial pairs for off, 155, 001, 003 and 046, separately by benchmark and super-family. Trials are averaged within questions, then questions receive equal weight. Each pair may retain different questions and trials; differences need not be transitive. Latest evaluable repairs replace original endpoints; repairs are not extra trials. Infrastructure failures remain missing; scientific missing submissions are zero. ASI comparisons require corrected judges on both sides; its absolute scores mix historical versions.

Colors indicate two-sided study-cluster sign-flip tests after Holm adjustment across 90 unique comparisons (10 pairs × 9 groups): green, positive adjusted p < .05; orange, negative adjusted p < .05; light gray, not significant; white, unavailable test. The diagonal is untested; mirrored cells share one test. Independent source-study blocks and joint sign symmetry under the null, or valid blockwise label exchangeability, are required. All questions and repeated trials within a block change sign together. At most 16 clusters are enumerated exactly; otherwise 99,999 random flips use a plus-one correction. Fewer than two clusters yields no test and contributes p=1 to the declared correction family. Missingness and nonrandom persona selection limit inference. Super-family means combine different native metrics; they are not pure field effects.

No comparison passes global Holm correction in this snapshot. This is insufficient evidence of differences, not evidence of equivalence. Results are exploratory, conditional on the observed trials; correction within this figure does not correct for prior persona selection. Numerical differences, raw/adjusted p values, question/episode/study counts, trial IDs and exact-versus-Monte-Carlo status are in `data/pairwise_selected/comparisons.csv`. Frozen snapshot hash and seed are in `summary.json`. Reproduce with `code/pairwise_performance.py`, then `code/plot_pairwise_performance.py`, or the last section of `performance_analysis.ipynb`.
