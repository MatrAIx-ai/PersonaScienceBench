**Word-based self-repetition, complete-fact recovery and failure coverage under five simulator settings.** Off, 155, 001, 003 and 046 use saved original Astra-low episodes; off/155/001 have three trials and 003/046 one. **A**, fraction of overlapping four-word sequences in a later message that appeared in an earlier same-role message of that conversation, averaged over eligible turns, trials within question, questions within benchmark and then equally across benchmarks. Filled circles represent simulator messages (81 common questions); hollow squares represent actor messages (80 common questions). Bars are pointwise 95% CIs. Case-normalized Unicode words are used; Han text is dictionary-segmented with jieba (HMM disabled). Initial/context messages, code fences and Markdown blockquotes are excluded. At least two length-eligible messages are required; role-specific panels can differ. This measures verbatim phrase reuse, not judged semantic redundancy or mode collapse. **B**, complete-fact recovery on 20 SciConv disambiguation cases, before intent gating. Dots are within-case percentages of runs recovering every annotated missing fact; violins smooth these discrete proportions with a Scott-bandwidth KDE restricted to observed ranges. White diamonds/bars show equal-case means/95% CIs. **D**, expected percentage of 40 common SciConv questions with at least one scientific failure among m episodes of each setting, averaging all size-m subsets without replacement. Off/155/001 support budgets 1–3; 003/046 only 1. Bands/error bars show 95% CIs. All CIs use 20,000 source-study bootstrap draws conditional on saved trials. Higher failure coverage is not higher task success. Different panels have different eligible populations.
