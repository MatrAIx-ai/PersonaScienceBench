## Candidate manuscript caption

**Persona-associated semantic separation of final responses and illustrative interaction trajectories.** **A,** Pairwise semantic separation among persona off and personas 155, 001, 004 and 046, calculated separately for simulator outputs (top) and actor outputs (bottom). Analysis uses 148 questions with complete original trial-1 interactions under all five settings, yielding 1,480 final role messages. Within each scientific super-family, each cell is the mean cross-persona cosine distance minus the average of the two within-persona distances, using different-question pairs for both quantities. The four super-families receive equal weight. Positive values indicate greater cross-persona than within-persona separation; within-persona variation here includes question differences, not repeated-rollout variability. Only the ten unique comparisons are displayed; the light-gray diagonal is omitted from testing and is zero by construction. Asterisks indicate one-sided question-wise persona-label-swap permutation tests with Holm-adjusted *p* < 0.05 across 20 comparisons (ten pairs × two roles; 10,000 permutations). Pointwise 95% confidence intervals from 20,000 question-bootstrap replicates stratified by source benchmark × super-family, and exact unadjusted and adjusted *p* values, are provided in the accompanying results table. **B,** User-selected examples from BixBench (`bix-57-q1`, displayed as “bix 57”) and SciConvBench (`matToolUse/disambiguation/case_236`, displayed as “case 236”) show all five settings from trial 1. Each node represents one generated role message; hollow diamonds mark final observed nodes. Colors consistently identify persona settings. UMAP is fitted jointly across all five settings and observed rounds, separately for each question and role. Curves connect consecutive observed nodes for visual clarity; intermediate positions do not represent observed states. Node coordinates are fixed, and neither arrows nor round-number labels are shown. All semantic representations use frozen Qwen3-Embedding-0.6B embeddings, L2-normalized before cosine-distance calculations. Local UMAP axes are not comparable across plots, and projected distances or path lengths are not effect sizes. These manually selected trajectories are illustrations, not independent statistical evidence; semantic distance is not a measure of task quality or realism.

## Exact analysis details for agents and manuscript editors

This handoff describes the **current** figure, superseding the earlier round-matched raw-distance heatmaps and PCA case studies. Explanatory text is intentionally outside the graphic; do not interpret its absence as a change to the methods.

- **Files:** main figure `figures/semantic/semantic.pdf` (also SVG, PNG and TIFF); executed notebook `semantic_trajectory_analysis.ipynb`; full results/methods `SEMANTIC_REPORT.md`; final-response numeric outputs `data/semantic_final/`; frozen trajectory coordinates `data/semantic/trajectory_coordinates.csv`; figure checks `figures/semantic/qa/`.
- **Personas and trial:** off/155/001/004/046, original Astra campaign, trial 1 only. Require complete data for all five settings and both roles on the same question set. No repaired, cancelled, Sol or later-trial outputs enter this analysis. No new model calls are needed to reproduce the figure.
- **Endpoint:** the last nonempty generated message for each role among the extracted simulator–actor exchanges in a complete episode. A simulator endpoint can be a closing message; an actor endpoint can be its last handoff, question or specification. This does not necessarily mean a successful task submission. Role endpoints may occur at different rounds. Panel B retains all observed messages, not just these final endpoints.
- **Coverage:** 148 questions: ASI 12, Bix 36, Discovery 29, EXP 34 and SciConv 37. Super-family counts are life sciences 41, physical/engineering 42, math/CS 41 and social/humanities 24. Each family contributes 25% to the overall statistic. Questions have equal weight within family; source weights within a family follow retained counts.
- **Embedding:** `Qwen/Qwen3-Embedding-0.6B`, revision `97b0c614be4d77ee51c0cef4e5f07c00f9eb65b3`; 1,024 dimensions, last-token pooling, L2 normalization. Strip outer whitespace only. Embed current generated text separately by role; exclude system prompts, injected persona profiles, metadata and repeated history. No summaries, translation, LLM judgment or message concatenation. Naturally generated styles and content remain and may affect distances.
- **Statistic:** for persona pair i,j and family f, average d(x_iq,x_js) over ordered q≠s to obtain B; obtain W_i and W_j with the same distinct-question rule within each persona. E_f=B−(W_i+W_j)/2; E is the mean of the four E_f values. Same-question distances never enter this statistic. The diagonal E_ii=0 by construction, not because within-persona distances vanish. Negative estimates are permitted. With normalized embeddings this statistic is sensitive to shifts in mean embeddings; it is not an omnibus detector of every distributional difference.
- **Null and permutation:** pairwise persona-label exchangeability within question. Independently swap the two tested labels for each question, retaining its outputs and benchmark/family identities; recompute the same domain-level statistic. Thus randomization preserves paired collection dependence even though the statistic uses only different-question distances. One-sided alternative E>0. Monte Carlo p=(1+# permuted statistics ≥ observed)/(10,000+1), with numerical comparison tolerance 1e−12. Seed 20261008; deterministic per-role/pair streams are recorded in `code/semantic/final_response.py`. Holm correction spans both roles, 20 tests total. A single star means adjusted p<0.05; no tiered star scheme.
- **Bootstrap:** 20,000 whole-question resamples within benchmark × family, retaining all five outputs together. If a question is sampled more than once, exclude pairs sharing the same original question; use multiplicity weights for the remaining pairs and renormalize. Percentile 95% intervals are pointwise, not multiplicity-adjusted, and need not agree with Holm decisions. Singleton strata have no within-stratum resampling variation.
- **Observed result:** nine of ten pairs show positive excess separation with adjusted p<0.05 in each role. Off versus 004 is not significant in either role (Holm p≈0.581). All 18 significant comparisons have Holm p≈0.002 at the chosen Monte Carlo resolution. Consult the CSV for exact estimates, intervals and p values; do not equate nonsignificance with equivalence.
- **Case choice:** the user manually selected these two cases after inspecting a gallery enriched for interactions with ≥4 complete exchanges in at least one setting. They were not sampled as representative cases. A completed exchange has both a simulator message and actor response at the same round; an unmatched simulator closing message is still plotted but does not count as a completed exchange. All five settings remain in each example, including shorter or single-node paths.
- **UMAP:** cosine metric, n_components=2, n_neighbors=min(15,N−1), min_dist=0.1, n_epochs=500, spectral initialization, random_state=42, transform_seed=42, n_jobs=1. Fit each question–role map to all observed trial-1 messages from all five settings. Frozen coordinates are copied exactly from the reviewed gallery; projection records are in `data/semantic/umap.json`. UMAP does not supply explained-variance percentages. The data-derived distances in A are calculated before projection.
- **Drawing:** curved quadratic connectors with control point offset 0.14 times the perpendicular chord in axis-normalized coordinates. Endpoints remain at observed UMAP coordinates. Curves are visual guides, not inferred intermediate semantic states. Final nodes use hollow diamonds. No arrows or point indices. Heatmaps show only the lower triangle; diagonal light gray, upper triangle white. Role/persona labels, a shared distance scale and persona legend remain. Case titles are “BixBench / bix 57” and “SciConvBench / case 236.” The figure is 183 × 145 mm, with editable vector text and ≥5.3 pt rendered text.
- **Limits:** inference is conditional on the observed trial, retained complete questions and exchangeability assumptions. Within-persona variability is across questions, not independent repeats of one question. Shared-source-study dependence beyond question is not modeled. Endpoint functions, lengths and styles vary. The persona panel, endpoint and case choices arose during exploratory work; this is not a preregistered confirmatory test. Do not claim causal persona effects, improved performance, measured realism, or complete distributional equality/inequality from this figure.
- **Reproduction:** `code/semantic/final_response.py` computes Panel A; `final_report.py` writes the report; `plot.py` draws the figure; `build_notebook.py` executes the notebook. Set notebook `RECOMPUTE=True` to recompute statistics from cached embeddings. `test_final_response.py` checks direct-distance equivalence under every label swap in a small example, bootstrap duplicate handling and Holm correction. The earlier `analyze.py`/`report.py` retain the superseded round-matched analysis and are not the current Panel A pipeline.
