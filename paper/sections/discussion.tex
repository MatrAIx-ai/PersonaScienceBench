\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}

Interactions with persona 155 illustrate how agents adapt their methods
and communication during multi-turn scientific work
(Figure~\ref{fig:discussion-cases}, Appendix~\ref{app:persona-profile}).
In BixBench, researcher feedback prompted the actor to replace an unavailable
R/limma~\cite{ritchie2015limma} workflow with Python analysis while retaining
the scientific objective. In SciConvBench, the actor switched from English
to Chinese and clarified requirements through five interaction iterations,
compared with one under persona off. Both SciConvBench attempts received
full credit, yet followed different communication and clarification paths.
Together, these cases show how agents can respond to execution constraints,
accommodate researcher preferences, and refine their understanding of the
task. Persona-based evaluation makes these aspects of scientific
collaboration visible alongside final scores, capturing how an agent works
with a particular researcher to pursue the task. Full interactions appear in
Appendices~\ref{app:case-workflow}, \ref{app:case-clarification},
and~\ref{app:case-clarification-off}.
