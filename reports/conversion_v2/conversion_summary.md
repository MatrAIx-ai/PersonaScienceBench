“Assets ready” checks local input/reference presence, hashes where declared, and file-role boundaries. It does not establish that every dependency, external service, or native evaluator works. Only the [two selected cases per benchmark](README.md) were exercised live.

- **BixBench:** all 59 referenced capsules were acquired; 60 cases remain blocked by unclassified input-file roles. They are cataloged but must not be treated as ready.
- **ASI-Bench:** all 60 prompt sets converted; only three instances have all required local data/reference assets. External solver or judge requirements still need runtime support.
- **ScienceAgentBench and ASI:** generated dependency recipes retain unresolved imports explicitly; asset readiness is not dependency validation.
- **SciConvBench:** three source cases lack a native request, intended specification, or issue annotations. The converted population does not reproduce the original paper’s prior-model-success filter.
- **EXP-Bench:** 20 question/reference alignment failures and one invalid/missing reference excluded. Of 440 design-only cases, 149 have permitted researcher facts and 291 have empty fact packs. Full experimental execution remains outside this track.

Catalogs are generated by `scripts/convert_benchmark_catalogs.py` under `data/conversion_catalog/`; their readiness flags remain conservative and do not incorporate selected live-run outcomes automatically.
