The problem considers steady, laminar, incompressible Couette flow of a Newtonian fluid between two infinite parallel flat plates.
The distance between the two plates is 10 mm.
The bottom plate is stationary at position y equals 0 with velocity u(0) equal to 0 m/s.
The top plate is located at position y equals 0.01 m moving at a constant speed of 5 m/s in the positive x direction.
The applied pressure gradient in the direction of flow is zero.
Solve the simplified Navier-Stokes equation d^2u/dy^2 equals 0 subject to the specified boundary conditions.
The resulting steady velocity profile is linear across the gap, defined by u(y) equals 500 * y, where y is the distance from the bottom plate in meters and u is the velocity in meters per second for y between 0 and 0.01 m.
