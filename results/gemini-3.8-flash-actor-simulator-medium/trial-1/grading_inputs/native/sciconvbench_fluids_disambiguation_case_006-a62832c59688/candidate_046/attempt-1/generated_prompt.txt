Model steady, incompressible, laminar Couette flow of a Newtonian fluid between two infinite parallel flat plates.
Set the separation distance between the lower and upper plates to 10 millimeters along the transverse y-axis.
Fix the lower plate at y equals 0 with a velocity of 0 meters per second.
Move the upper plate at y equals 10 millimeters with a constant velocity of 5 meters per second in the positive x-direction.
Set the streamwise pressure gradient to zero.
Apply the no-slip boundary condition at both plate surfaces.
Solve the simplified Navier-Stokes momentum equation to obtain the linear steady velocity profile u(y) = 500 * y meters per second, where y is measured in meters from the lower plate.
