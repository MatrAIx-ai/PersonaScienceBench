Model steady laminar Couette flow of an incompressible Newtonian fluid between two infinite parallel plates.
Set the plate separation distance to 10 millimeters along the y-axis from y = 0 to y = 0.01 meters.
Set the bottom plate at y = 0 to be stationary with a velocity of 0 meters per second.
Set the top plate at y = 0.01 meters to move in the positive x-direction with a constant velocity of 5 meters per second.
Apply a zero pressure gradient along the flow direction.
Solve the one-dimensional Navier-Stokes momentum equation under these boundary conditions to determine the velocity profile.
Provide the resulting velocity profile as a linear equation u(y) = 500 * y meters per second for y in the range from 0 to 0.01 meters.
