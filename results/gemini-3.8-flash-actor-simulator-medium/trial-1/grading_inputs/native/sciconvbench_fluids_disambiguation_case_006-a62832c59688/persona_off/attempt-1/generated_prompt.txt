Model steady, laminar, incompressible Couette flow of a Newtonian fluid between two infinite parallel plates.
Set the distance between the bottom plate and the top plate to 10 millimeters along the vertical y-axis.
Set the velocity of the bottom plate at y equals 0 to 0 meters per second.
