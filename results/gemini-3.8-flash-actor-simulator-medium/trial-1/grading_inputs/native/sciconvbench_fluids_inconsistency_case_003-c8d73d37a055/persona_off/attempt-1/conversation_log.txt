Q: What is the inlet temperature?
A: An explicit inlet temperature isn't specified in the problem. 

You can assume quasi-1D isentropic flow with $\gamma = 1.4$ and a constant stagnation temperature, and express the change or exit velocity either as a ratio/percentage compared to the inlet velocity ($V_2/V_1$), or in terms of $T_1$ (or standard room temperature, e.g., $T_1 = 300\text{ K}$, if you need a concrete value).
