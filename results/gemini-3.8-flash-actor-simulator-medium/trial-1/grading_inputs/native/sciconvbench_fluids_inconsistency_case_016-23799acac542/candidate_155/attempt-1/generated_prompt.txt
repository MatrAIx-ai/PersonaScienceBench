Model the radial pressure gradient using the differential equation dP/dr equals rho times omega squared times r.
Treat the fluid density rho as an unspecified symbolic constant.
Integrate the radial pressure gradient with respect to radius r.
Express the general pressure distribution as P(r) equals P_0 plus 0.5 times rho times omega squared times (r squared minus r_0 squared), where P_0 is the pressure at reference radius r_0.
