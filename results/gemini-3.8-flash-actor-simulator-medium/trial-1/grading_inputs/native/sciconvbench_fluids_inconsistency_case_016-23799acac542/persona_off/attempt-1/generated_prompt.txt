Calculate the radial pressure distribution P(r) for an incompressible fluid inside a centrifuge spinning at 10,000 RPM.
Convert the rotation speed of 10,000 RPM into angular velocity omega in radians per second.
Use the radial equilibrium differential equation dP/dr = rho * r * omega^2.
Treat the fluid density rho as an independent variable or evaluate it using water with a density of 1000 kg/m^3 for concrete demonstrations.
Integrate the differential equation using a reference boundary condition where pressure equals P_0 at a reference radius r_0.
Express the resulting radial pressure distribution analytically as P(r) = P_0 + 0.5 * rho * omega^2 * (r^2 - r_0^2).
