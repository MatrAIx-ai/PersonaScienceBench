The task is to calculate the isentropic stagnation flow properties for a subsonic flow.
Use an upstream Mach number of 0.5.
Model the working fluid as an ideal gas with a specific heat ratio gamma equal to 1.4 representing air.
Apply the standard subsonic isentropic flow relations rather than the Rayleigh-Pitot formula.
Determine the analytical formulas and numerical values for the total-to-static pressure ratio.
Determine the analytical formulas and numerical values for the total-to-static temperature ratio.
Determine the analytical formulas and numerical values for the total-to-static density ratio.
Present the resulting dimensionless stagnation property ratios evaluated at Mach 0.5.
