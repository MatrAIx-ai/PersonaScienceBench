The simulation models a two-dimensional laminar incompressible lid-driven cavity flow using OpenFOAM.
The solver is icoFoam.
The computational domain is a square cavity of dimensions 0.1 m by 0.1 m with a depth of 0.01 m in the z-direction.
The mesh is generated using blockMesh with a uniform discretization of 20 cells along the x-axis, 20 cells along the y-axis, and 1 cell along the z-axis.
The kinematic viscosity nu is set to 0.001 m2/s in constant/transportProperties to achieve a Reynolds number of 100.
The top boundary is configured as a moving wall with a velocity of 1 m/s in the x-direction and zero gradient for pressure.
The bottom, left, and right boundaries are configured as stationary no-slip walls with zero velocity and zero gradient for pressure.
The front and back boundaries are defined as empty patches to enforce two-dimensional flow.
Initial velocity field is set to zero across the entire internal field.
Initial kinematic pressure field is set to zero across the entire internal field.
The simulation start time is 0 seconds and end time is 0.5 seconds.
The time step deltaT is set to 0.002 seconds.
The write control is set to timeStep with a write interval of 20 time steps.
