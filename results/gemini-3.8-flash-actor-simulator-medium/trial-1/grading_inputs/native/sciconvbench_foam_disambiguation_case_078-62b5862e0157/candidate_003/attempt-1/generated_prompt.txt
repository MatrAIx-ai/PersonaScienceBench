The simulation uses the OpenFOAM solver interFoam to model gravity-driven transient sloshing in a rectangular tank.
The tank geometry has dimensions of 1.0 m in the x direction, 0.6 m in the y direction, and 0.1 m in the z direction.
The mesh is generated using blockMesh with 200 cells along x, 120 cells along y, and 1 cell along z.
The front and back patches are configured with the empty boundary condition for a two-dimensional simulation.
The bottom and side walls are modeled as no-slip walls with fixedValue (0 0 0) for velocity, fixedFluxPressure for p_rgh, and zeroGradient for alpha.water.
The top boundary is open to the atmosphere with pressureInletOutletVelocity for velocity, totalPressure with p0 equal to 0 for p_rgh, and inletOutlet with inletValue equal to 0 for alpha.water.
Gravity is directed in the negative y direction with vector (0 -9.81 0).
The fluid is modeled as laminar using simulationType laminar.
Standard physical properties for water and air are applied.
The initial fluid velocity is uniform zero and initial p_rgh is set to zero.
Sloshing is initiated by an initial tilted free surface varying linearly from y equal to 0.25 m at x equal to 0 to y equal to 0.35 m at x equal to 1.0 m.
The initial water phase distribution is defined in setFields using a default value of alpha.water equal to 0 and ten staircase boxToCell regions across x with widths of 0.1 m and heights stepping from 0.255 m to 0.345 m.
The total simulation time is 10 seconds.
The initial deltaT is 0.001 seconds with adjustTimeStep enabled, a maximum Courant number of 0.5, and a writeInterval of 0.05 seconds.
