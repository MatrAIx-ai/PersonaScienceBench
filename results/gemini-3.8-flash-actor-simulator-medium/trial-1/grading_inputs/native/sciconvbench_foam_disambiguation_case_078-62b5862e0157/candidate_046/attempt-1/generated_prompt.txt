Simulate transient, gravity-driven sloshing of water and air in OpenFOAM using the interFoam solver.
Model the computational domain as a rectangular tank with a length of 1.0 m in x, a height of 0.6 m in y, and a depth of 0.1 m in z.
Generate a structured mesh using blockMesh with 200 cells in x, 120 cells in y, and 1 cell in z for a quasi-2D configuration.
Set the front and back boundary patches to the empty boundary condition type.
Set the bottom, left, and right boundary patches as no-slip rigid walls.
Configure the top boundary as open to the atmosphere with pressureInletOutletVelocity for velocity, totalPressure with p0 set to 0 Pa for p_rgh, and inletOutlet with an inletValue of 0 for alpha.water.
Set the gravitational acceleration vector to (0 -9.81 0) m/s^2 in constant/g.
Set the simulation turbulence regime to laminar in constant/momentumTransport or constant/turbulenceProperties.
Use standard fluid transport properties for water and air with a surface tension coefficient of 0.072 N/m.
Initialize the fluid velocity field to uniform (0 0 0) m/s.
Initialize the water volume fraction alpha.water with a tilted free surface having a water depth of 0.25 m at x = 0 m and linearly increasing to 0.35 m at x = 1.0 m.
Set the total simulation physical run time to 10 seconds.
Set the initial time step deltaT to 0.001 seconds with automatic time step adjustment enabled.
Set the maximum Courant number maxCo to 0.5.
Set the results writeInterval to 0.05 seconds.
