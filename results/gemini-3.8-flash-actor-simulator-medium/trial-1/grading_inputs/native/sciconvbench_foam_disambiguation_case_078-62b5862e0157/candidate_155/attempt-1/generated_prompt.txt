The simulation shall be conducted using the OpenFOAM solver interFoam.
The computational domain is a rectangular tank with a length of 1.0 meter in the x direction, a height of 0.6 meters in the y direction, and a depth of 0.1 meters in the z direction.
The computational mesh shall be generated using blockMesh with 200 cells in the x direction, 120 cells in the y direction, and 20 cells in the z direction.
The tank is half-filled with water having an initial free surface inclined linearly from y = 0.25 meters at x = 0 meters to y = 0.35 meters at x = 1.0 meters.
The initial velocity field throughout the domain is zero meters per second.
Gravitational acceleration is set to 9.81 meters per second squared acting in the negative y direction.
The turbulence model is set to laminar in the momentum transport properties.
The top boundary of the tank is open to the atmosphere.
The boundary condition for velocity at the top boundary is pressureInletOutletVelocity.
The boundary condition for modified pressure p_rgh at the top boundary is totalPressure with p0 set to 0.
The boundary condition for the water phase fraction alpha.water at the top boundary is inletOutlet with inletValue set to 0.
The bottom and side boundaries are modeled as no-slip solid walls.
The front and back boundaries are modeled as no-slip solid walls.
The total simulation time is 10 seconds.
The initial time step size deltaT is 0.001 seconds.
Adaptive time stepping adjustTimeStep is enabled.
The maximum Courant number maxCo is set to 0.5.
The data write interval writeInterval is set to 0.05 seconds.
