Simulate spray combustion of liquid dodecane in OpenFOAM using sprayFoam.
Model the computational domain as a two-dimensional axisymmetric cylindrical chamber with an axial length of 0.2 meters and a diameter of 0.1 meters.

Define droplet sizes using a Rosin-Rammler distribution with a mean droplet diameter of 50 micrometers.
Use the Ranz-Marshall model for droplet heat and mass transfer.
Use the standard drag model for droplet momentum transfer.
Determine droplet injection velocity consistently from the standard cone injection kinematics.
Implement a two-step global reaction mechanism for dodecane combustion configured in constant/combustionProperties and constant/reactionSchemes.
