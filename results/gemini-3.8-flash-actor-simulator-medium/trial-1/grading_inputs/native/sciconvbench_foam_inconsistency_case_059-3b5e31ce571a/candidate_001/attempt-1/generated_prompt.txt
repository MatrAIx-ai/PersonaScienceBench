Simulate steady-state two-phase oil and water flow in a horizontal pipe using the twoPhaseEulerFoam solver in OpenFOAM.
Model the pipe geometry with a circular cross-section having an inner diameter of 0.05 meters and a length of 1.0 meter aligned horizontally along the x-axis.
Apply a fixed inlet mixture velocity of 1.0 meter per second oriented along the pipe axis for both phases.
Set the water phase volume fraction alpha.water at the inlet to a uniform fixed value of 0.3.
Set the oil phase volume fraction alpha.oil at the inlet to a uniform fixed value of 0.7.
Apply zeroGradient boundary conditions for alpha.water and alpha.oil at the pipe outlet.
Apply a fixed uniform pressure of 0 Pascal gauge at the outlet for p_rgh.
Apply zeroGradient boundary conditions for phase velocities at the outlet.
Apply noSlip boundary conditions for both phases on the pipe wall surfaces.
Apply zeroGradient boundary conditions for phase volume fractions and p_rgh on the pipe wall surfaces.
Specify standard physical fluid properties for water with density of 1000 kilograms per cubic meter and kinematic viscosity of 1e-6 square meters per second.
Specify physical fluid properties for oil with density of 850 kilograms per cubic meter and kinematic viscosity of 1e-5 square meters per second.
Run the simulation until residuals converge to establish steady-state flow characteristics.
