The simulation models 2D natural convection in a square cavity according to the de Vahl Davis benchmark using OpenFOAM.
The OpenFOAM solver is buoyantBoussinesqPimpleFoam.
The cavity geometry is a 2D square with a side length of 0.1 m in the x and y directions and a depth of 0.01 m in the z direction.
The computational domain is discretized using a uniform structured mesh of 80 cells along the x-axis, 80 cells along the y-axis, and 1 cell along the z-axis.
The front and back boundaries are set to the empty patch type for a 2D simulation.
The gravitational acceleration vector is set to (0 -9.81 0) m/s2.
The fluid is air modeled using the Boussinesq approximation with an operating temperature of 295 K.
The left wall boundary conditions are noSlip for velocity and fixedValue of 300 K for temperature.
The right wall boundary conditions are noSlip for velocity and fixedValue of 290 K for temperature.
The top wall boundary conditions are noSlip for velocity and zeroGradient for temperature.
The bottom wall boundary conditions are noSlip for velocity and zeroGradient for temperature.
The pressure field p_rgh has zeroGradient or fixedFluxPressure boundary conditions on all wall boundaries.
The simulation is run transiently until reaching steady-state heat transfer and flow conditions.
