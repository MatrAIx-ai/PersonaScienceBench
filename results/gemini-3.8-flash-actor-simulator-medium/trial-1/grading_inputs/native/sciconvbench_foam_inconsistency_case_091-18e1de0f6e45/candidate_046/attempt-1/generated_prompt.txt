The simulation domain is a two-dimensional square cavity with side length 0.1 m aligned with the x and y axes.
The mesh is structured and uniform with 80 cells in the x direction, 80 cells in the y direction, and 1 cell in the z direction.
The front and back boundaries along the z axis are set to empty boundary conditions to enforce two-dimensional flow.
The working fluid is air modeled using the Boussinesq approximation.
The reference temperature is set to 295 K.
The gravitational acceleration vector is set to (0, -9.81, 0) m/s^2.
The thermophysical properties of air are evaluated at the reference temperature of 295 K.
The left wall boundary conditions are fixedValue temperature of 300 K, no-slip velocity, and zeroGradient for modified pressure p_rgh.
The right wall boundary conditions are fixedValue temperature of 290 K, no-slip velocity, and zeroGradient for modified pressure p_rgh.
The top wall boundary conditions are zeroGradient temperature, no-slip velocity, and zeroGradient for modified pressure p_rgh.
The bottom wall boundary conditions are zeroGradient temperature, no-slip velocity, and zeroGradient for modified pressure p_rgh.
The OpenFOAM solver used for the simulation is buoyantBoussinesqPimpleFoam.
The simulation is advanced in time with adjustable time stepping restricted by a maximum Courant number of 0.5 until steady state is reached.
Convergence to steady state is achieved when all field residuals fall below 1e-6.
