The objective is to estimate the in-plane thermal-mismatch strain induced in an aluminum thin film bonded to a ceramic substrate after cooling.
Use a temperature change of negative 180 Kelvin.
Use a substrate coefficient of thermal expansion of 8e-6 per Kelvin.
Use an aluminum film coefficient of thermal expansion of 23e-6 per Kelvin.
Assume the thin-film condition where the substrate is substantially thicker and stiffer than the film.
Calculate the thermal-mismatch strain as the difference between substrate and film thermal expansion coefficients multiplied by the temperature change.
Report the resulting thermal-mismatch strain in the film as positive 2.7e-3, representing a tensile strain of 0.27 percent.
