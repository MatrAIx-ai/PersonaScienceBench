The task is to perform a variable-cell relaxation followed by an SCF total-energy calculation for ferroelectric BiFeO3 in the R3c space group.
Build an initial 10-atom primitive rhombohedral unit cell of BiFeO3 consistent with the R3c space group containing 2 Bi, 2 Fe, and 6 O atoms using standard literature lattice constants.
Assign collinear spin polarization with G-type antiferromagnetic ordering between the two Fe sublattices by setting opposite starting magnetizations for the Fe atoms.
Use the specified local LDA pseudopotentials from PseudoDojo SR_v0.4.1 located at PseudoDojo/SR_v0.4.1/LDA_standard/bi.upf, PseudoDojo/SR_v0.4.1/LDA_standard/fe.upf, and PseudoDojo/SR_v0.4.1/LDA_standard/o.upf.
Set the kinetic energy cutoff for wavefunctions to 80 Ry and the charge density cutoff to 320 Ry.
Use an automatic Monkhorst-Pack k-point grid of 4x4x4 with a half shift of 1 1 1.
Set the self-consistent field electronic convergence threshold conv_thr to 1.0e-8 Ry.
Set the total energy convergence threshold for structural relaxation etot_conv_thr to 1.0e-8 Ry.
Set the force convergence threshold forc_conv_thr to 1.0e-7 Ry per atomic unit.
Execute the variable-cell relaxation calculation to optimize both the lattice vectors and atomic coordinates until convergence criteria are met.
Extract the optimized lattice parameters and atomic positions from the completed variable-cell relaxation calculation.
Perform a self-consistent field total-energy calculation on the fully relaxed structure using the identical pseudopotentials, cutoffs, k-point grid, and convergence thresholds.
Output the fully relaxed structural geometry and the final SCF total energy.
