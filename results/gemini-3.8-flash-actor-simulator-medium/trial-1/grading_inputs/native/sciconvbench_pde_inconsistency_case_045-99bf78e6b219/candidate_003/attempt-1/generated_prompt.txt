Apply periodic boundary conditions in the y-direction across y = 0 and y = 1.

Format the solution array u with shape (len(y), len(x)) such that u[j, i] = u(x[i], y[j]).

Generate and save a heatmap or contour plot of u(x, y, T) to a file named solution_agent_heat_16.png.
