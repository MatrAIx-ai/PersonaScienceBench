The shaft has a uniform solid circular cross section of diameter D, radius R, and total length L.
The material is homogeneous, isotropic, and obeys linear-elastic constitutive behavior governed by shear modulus G and Young modulus E.
Deformations remain small such that Saint-Venant torsion theory and linear beam shear theory apply.
Torques are applied at N discrete cross sections along the longitudinal axis x at positions x_i for i from 1 to N.
Transverse shear loading is applied across the shaft cross section producing an internal resultant shear force V(x).
The internal torque T(x) at any axial coordinate x is determined by the piecewise equilibrium of all applied external torques up to x.
The polar moment of inertia of the solid circular cross section is J equals pi multiplied by D to the fourth power divided by 32.
The cross-sectional area is A equals pi multiplied by D squared divided by 4.
The second moment of area about the bending neutral axis is I equals pi multiplied by D to the fourth power divided by 64.
The shear stress due to torsion at radial distance r from the shaft center is tau_torsion(r, x) equals T(x) multiplied by r divided by J.
The maximum shear stress due to torsion occurs at the outer surface r equals R and is tau_max_torsion(x) equals 16 multiplied by T(x) divided by pi multiplied by D cubed.
The shear stress distribution due to transverse shear force V(x) across the solid circular cross section follows Jourawski beam shear formula tau_shear(y, x) equals V(x) multiplied by Q(y) divided by the product of I and the chord width b(y).
The maximum shear stress due to transverse shear occurs at the neutral axis where chord width equals D and is tau_max_shear(x) equals 4 multiplied by V(x) divided by 3 multiplied by A.
The local rate of twist per unit length along the shaft is d_phi_dx equals T(x) divided by the product of G and J.
The total angle of twist phi(x) relative to a reference cross section is obtained by integrating T(x) divided by the product of G and J along the shaft length.
For segments with piecewise constant torque T_k over segment length L_k, the segment twist angle is delta_phi_k equals T_k multiplied by L_k divided by the product of G and J.
The cumulative angle of twist at any station is the algebraic sum of the twist angles of all preceding segments from the fixed support.
At any point on the outer surface where transverse shear stress is zero, the principal stresses under pure torsion are sigma_1 equals tau_torsion and sigma_2 equals negative tau_torsion.
When combined normal bending stresses sigma(x) accompany the torque and transverse shear, the maximum in-plane shear stress at the critical outer fiber is tau_max equals the square root of the quantity half of sigma squared plus tau_torsion squared.
The equivalent von Mises stress for combined normal and torsional shear stress at the critical surface fiber is sigma_vm equals the square root of the quantity sigma squared plus 3 multiplied by tau_torsion squared.
