Q: Do you require a general analytical derivation of the diameter formula, or a numerical calculation using specific values?
A: Specific values ke saath numerical calculation chahiye. 

Yeh values use karke minimum required diameter $d$ calculate karna hai:

- **Units:** N–mm–MPa
- **Shaft length ($L$):** $1500\text{ mm}$
- **Material:** 6061-T6 aluminum
- **Shear modulus ($G$):** $25939.8\text{ MPa}$
- **Torque ($T$):** $1000000\text{ N}\cdot\text{mm}$
- **Angle of twist limit ($\phi_{\text{allow}}$):** $0.75^\circ$ (pure length $L$ ke liye)
- **Governing relation:** $\phi = \frac{TL}{JG}$, jahan $J = \frac{\pi d^4}{32}$
