The objective is to calculate the minimum required solid circular shaft diameter based on an angle of twist limit under pure torsion.
Assume small-deformation linear-elastic isotropic solid mechanics behavior.
The shaft has length L equal to 1500 mm.
The shaft material is aluminum 6061-T6 with shear modulus G equal to 25939.8 MPa.
The applied torque T is equal to 1000000 N mm.
The allowable angle of twist over length L is 0.75 degrees.
Convert the allowable twist angle to radians using the factor pi divided by 180, giving approximately 0.01309 radians.
Use the governing elastic torsional deflection relation phi equals T multiplied by L divided by the product of J and G.
Use the polar moment of inertia relation for a solid circular cross-section J equals pi multiplied by d to the fourth power divided by 32.
Formulate the minimum diameter relation as d equals the fourth root of 32 multiplied by T multiplied by L, divided by the product of pi, G, and allowable twist angle in radians.
Compute the resulting minimum shaft diameter as approximately 81.90 mm.
