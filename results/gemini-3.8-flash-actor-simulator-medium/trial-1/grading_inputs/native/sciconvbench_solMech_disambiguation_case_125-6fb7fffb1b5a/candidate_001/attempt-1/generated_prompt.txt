The task is to calculate the bending stress distribution for a linear-elastic, isotropic rectangular beam cross-section under pure bending.
The cross-section width b is 50 mm.
The cross-section height h is 120 mm.
The centroidal axis is the neutral axis, with vertical coordinate y measured positive upward from the neutral axis.
The second moment of area about the neutral axis is calculated using I = (b * h^3) / 12.
The internal bending moment applied to the section is M = 4,000,000 N·mm, defined as a positive sagging bending moment.
The governing relation for bending normal stress is sigma(y) = - (M * y) / I under standard sign conventions where positive moment induces compression on the top fibers and tension on the bottom fibers.
Calculate the bending normal stress at the neutral axis where y = 0 mm.
Calculate the bending normal stress at the top fiber where y = +60 mm.
Calculate the bending normal stress at the bottom fiber where y = -60 mm.
State all results in units of MPa alongside the governing relations and standard assumptions of Euler-Bernoulli beam theory.
