The task is to calculate the bending stress distribution for a linear-elastic rectangular beam cross-section.
The beam width is 50 mm and the beam height is 120 mm.
The coordinate y is measured along the vertical axis from the centroidal neutral axis positive upwards.
The cross-section centroid is located at half the height, setting the bottom fiber at y = -60 mm and the top fiber at y = 60 mm.
The internal bending moment applied to the section is 4,000,000 N·mm sagging.
Small-deformation linear-elastic Euler-Bernoulli beam theory applies.
The area moment of inertia about the horizontal neutral axis is calculated as (50 * 120^3) / 12 = 7,200,000 mm^4.
For a sagging moment, top fibers experience compression (negative normal stress) and bottom fibers experience tension (positive normal stress) under the standard relation sigma = -M * y / I.
Compute the normal bending stress at the neutral axis y = 0 mm, yielding 0.00 MPa.
Compute the normal bending stress at the top fiber y = 60 mm, yielding -33.33 MPa.
Compute the normal bending stress at the bottom fiber y = -60 mm, yielding +33.33 MPa.
Present all calculation steps, intermediate geometric values, and final stress outputs in units of N, mm, and MPa.
