Q: What is the exact mathematical formula and amplitude for the sinusoidal vertical traction applied to the hypotenuse?
A: The vertical traction applied on the hypotenuse is given by:

$$\sigma_{yy} = -5\times 10^3 \sin(\pi x)\text{ Pa}$$

So the amplitude is $5\times 10^3\text{ Pa}$ (acting in the negative vertical direction as $-5\text{e}3 \sin(\pi x)$).
