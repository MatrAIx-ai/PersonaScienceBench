Q: What finite element family and polynomial degree should be used for the vector displacement field?
A: The requirement does not specify a mandatory element family or polynomial degree beyond using a standard vector-valued displacement space. 

Standard continuous Lagrange elements—such as standard linear (`P1`) or quadratic (`P2`) vector elements (e.g., `VectorFunctionSpace(mesh, "Lagrange", 1)` or degree 2)—are typical and perfectly fine to use. Degree 1 or 2 Lagrange elements are standard for linear elasticity in FEniCS.
