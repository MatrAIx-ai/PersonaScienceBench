Use legacy FEniCS (dolfin) to solve a 2D small-strain linear elasticity problem under plane stress.
Define the geometry as a rectangular domain with x from 0.0 to 1.0 m and y from 0.0 to 0.20 m.
Discretize the domain using a uniform structured rectangle mesh with 20 subdivisions along x and 4 subdivisions along y.
Define a degree 1 continuous vector Lagrange function space for the displacement field u.
Set the material properties with Young's modulus E equal to 200 GPa and Poisson's ratio nu equal to 0.30.
Apply the reduced plane-stress constitutive law with effective Lamé parameter lambda equal to E * nu / (1 - nu^2) and shear modulus mu equal to E / (2 * (1 + nu)).
Assume unit plate thickness.
Apply a Dirichlet boundary condition fixing displacement u equal to (0.0, 0.0) m on the left boundary at x = 0.0.
Apply a Dirichlet boundary condition setting displacement u equal to (0.001, 0.0) m on the right boundary at x = 1.0.
Leave the top boundary at y = 0.20 and bottom boundary at y = 0.0 traction-free.
Solve the linear variational problem using a direct linear solver.
