The implementation uses legacy FEniCS (dolfin).
The geometry is a rectangular domain of size 1.0 m by 0.20 m with two circular holes of radius 0.04 m centered at (0.40, 0.10) m and (0.60, 0.10) m.
A triangular mesh resolving the holes and domain boundaries is generated with a suitable resolution using mshr or equivalent mesh generation.
The problem is modeled under plane strain conditions using a large-deformation isotropic quasi-incompressible neo-Hookean formulation.
The mixed finite element formulation employs displacement u and hydrostatic pressure p using stable elements such as Taylor-Hood (P2-P1).
The material properties are Young modulus E = 5.0 MPa and Poisson ratio nu = 0.49.
The left edge at x = 0 is fully clamped with u_x = 0 and u_y = 0.
The right edge at x = 1.0 has prescribed displacement u_x = 0.060 m and u_y = 0.
The top and bottom edges are traction-free.
Both circular hole boundaries are subjected to a uniform pressure of 0.10 MPa applied with respect to the reference normals of the undeformed holes.
The nonlinear coupled system is solved using Newton method.
The hydrostatic pressure field p is plotted and saved as q14_p.png.
The von Mises stress computed from the Cauchy stress tensor is plotted and saved as q14_vm.png.
The resulting displacement field is exported and saved in XDMF format.
