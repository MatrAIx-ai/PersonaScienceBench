Use legacy FEniCS (dolfin) to solve a quasi-incompressible finite-strain hyperelasticity boundary value problem in plane strain.
Formulate the governing equations using a mixed displacement-pressure neo-Hookean hyperelastic model.
Solve the coupled mixed system using Newton's method.
Define the domain as a rectangular strip from x = 0 to x = 1.0 m and y = 0 to y = 0.20 m containing two circular holes of radius 0.04 m centered at (0.40, 0.10) m and (0.60, 0.10) m.
Set the material parameters to Young's modulus E = 5 MPa and Poisson's ratio nu = 0.49.
Discretize the mixed displacement and pressure fields using an inf-sup stable finite element pair.
Constrain the left edge at x = 0 with zero horizontal and vertical displacements.
Prescribe the right edge at x = 1.0 m with a horizontal displacement of 0.060 m and zero vertical displacement.
Apply a uniform pressure of 0.10 MPa to each hole boundary oriented along the reference normals of the undeformed holes.
Maintain traction-free boundary conditions on the top and bottom edges.
