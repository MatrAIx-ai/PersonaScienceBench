Steady, fully developed, incompressible Couette flow between two infinite parallel plates.
The fluid is Newtonian with constant viscosity, and gravity is neglected.
The gap between the plates (distance) is 10 mm.
Define a Cartesian coordinate system where y is measured from the bottom plate (y = 0) toward the top plate (y = 10 mm), and x is the flow direction.
The bottom plate (y = 0) is stationary with velocity u = 0.
The top plate (y = 10 mm) moves in the positive x-direction with velocity U = 5 m/s.
No-slip boundary conditions apply at both plates.
The pressure gradient along the flow direction is zero: ∂p/∂x = 0.
Apply the x-momentum Navier–Stokes equation, which reduces to d²u/dy² = 0 under these assumptions.
Integrate twice and apply the boundary conditions to obtain the velocity profile.
The steady velocity profile is linear: u(y) = U·y/d = 500·y (m/s), where y is in meters.
Express the result such that u(0) = 0, u(0.01 m) = 5 m/s, and u(0.005 m) = 2.5 m/s.
Report the velocity profile as a function of y, including the shear rate du/dy = 500 s⁻¹ and the shear stress τ = μ·500 Pa (expressed in terms of the dynamic viscosity μ).
