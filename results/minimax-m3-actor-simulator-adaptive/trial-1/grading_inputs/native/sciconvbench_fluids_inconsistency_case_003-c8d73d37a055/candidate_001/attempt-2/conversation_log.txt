Q: You mentioned Mach 2.0 air entering a converging nozzle. For supersonic flow (Mach > 1), a converging passage physically acts as a diffuser and decelerates the flow, whereas a diverging passage accelerates it. Do you want to treat this as an isentropic diffuser/duct (correcting the deceleration), or did you intend the inlet to be subsonic with the nozzle accelerating it? Please clarify the intended setup.
A: Haan bhai, bilkul supersonic hi hai — Mach 2.0 inlet pe. Converging nozzle hai (A2/A1 = 0.7), toh supersonic flow ke liye ye diffuser ki tarah behave karega aur slow hogi. Supersonic branch pe hi area-Mach relation use karna hai, isentropic, gamma = 1.4, stagnation temperature constant. 

Bas exit Mach number aur exit velocity nikal do. Aur haan, velocity actually *decrease* hogi, increase nahi — original question mein "increase" likha tha wo galat hai supersonic + converging case ke liye. ΔV negative aayega (ya magnitude mein deceleration bata do, jaise bhi present karna ho).
