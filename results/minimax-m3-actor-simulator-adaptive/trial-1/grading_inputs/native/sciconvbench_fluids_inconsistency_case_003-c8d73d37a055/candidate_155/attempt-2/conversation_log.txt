Q: I need to clarify a physical conflict in the problem before proceeding. At supersonic inlet conditions (Mach 2.0), a converging duct (A2/A1 < 1) acts as a diffuser and physically decelerates the flow, so the exit velocity would be lower than the inlet velocity, not higher.

Which scenario do you want me to model?

1. Isentropic supersonic flow throughout (flow decelerates from Mach 2.0 in the converging passage; I will report the actual velocity change).

2. A normal shock occurs inside the nozzle, after which the flow becomes subsonic and is further accelerated by the remaining converging section (I need throat area or shock location to solve this).

3. The problem was meant to specify a diverging nozzle (i.e., A2/A1 > 1, such that supersonic flow accelerates).
A: 请使用**场景1：等熵超声速流动**。

完整要求的参数已明确给出：

- 准一维等熵流动（不考虑激波）
- 比热比 γ = 1.4
- 恒定滞止温度
- 使用面积-马赫数关系式，**取超声速分支**（马赫数大于1的那一支）

关于题目中"出口速度的增加"这一表述——实际上是不准确的。完整要求中已注明：**超声速流动在收缩通道中面积减小会导致减速，马赫数减小**。所以你应该计算实际的速度变化，结果应为减速（出口速度小于入口速度），即"增加"为负值。

入口条件：M₁ = 2.0，面积比 A₂/A₁ = 0.7。请直接按面积-马赫数公式在超声速分支上求解出口马赫数 M₂，再结合恒定滞止温度求出口速度，并指出实际是速度降低。
