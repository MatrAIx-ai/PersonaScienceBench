Q: Symbolic expression chahiye (rho ko variable rakh ke), ya numerical pressure values bhi nikaalne hain kisi specific radii pe? Agar numerical chahiye, toh inner aur outer radius specify kar dena.
A: Haan bhai, symbolic expression hi nikaal do. Complete requirement mein koi specific radius mentioned nahi hai - na inner, na outer. Toh general form chahiye:

P(r) = (1/2)*rho*omega^2*r^2 + C

Bas itna. Integration constant C ke liye boundary condition mention kar dena - jaise ki free surface pe P = P_atm, ya axis pe (r=0) reference pressure. Yeh standard approach hai rotating fluid problems ke liye.

Agar koi numerical example dena ho toh omega = 10000 RPM = 2*pi*10000/60 ≈ 1047.2 rad/s le lena, aur water ka density (rho = 1000 kg/m³) example ke liye use kar sakte ho. But core requirement toh symbolic distribution hai, wohi show karo primarily.

Aur ek baat - P = rho*g*h wala formula static fluids ke liye hai, rotating case mein galat hai. Centripetal acceleration r*omega^2 hai jo radius ke saath linearly badhta hai, isliye pressure parabolic hota hai r mein (quadratic), na ki linear. Yeh point bhi clearly mention karna answer mein.
