Derive the radial pressure distribution for a fluid in a centrifuge using the rotating-frame equilibrium equation dP/dr = ρ·r·ω².
Use water as the working fluid with density ρ = 1000 kg/m³.
Use the angular velocity ω = 2π·(10000/60) ≈ 1047.2 rad/s corresponding to a rotational speed of 10,000 RPM.
Apply the boundary condition P(r = 0) = P₀, where P₀ is atmospheric pressure of 101,325 Pa (free surface / center line vented to atmosphere).
Integrate dP/dr = ρ·r·ω² analytically from r = 0 to an arbitrary radius r to obtain the symbolic profile P(r) = P₀ + ½·ρ·ω²·r².
Evaluate P(r) for the default rotor inner radius R = 0.1 m and report the numerical pressure value at the wall (r = R), as well as the gauge pressure ΔP = P(R) − P₀.
Repeat the wall-pressure evaluation parametrically for R = 0.15 m and R = 0.2 m for comparison.
Present the final results with symbol definitions, the integrated formula, a table of P(R) for the three radii, and an explicit note that the static formula P = ρ·g·h does not apply in this rotating geometry.
