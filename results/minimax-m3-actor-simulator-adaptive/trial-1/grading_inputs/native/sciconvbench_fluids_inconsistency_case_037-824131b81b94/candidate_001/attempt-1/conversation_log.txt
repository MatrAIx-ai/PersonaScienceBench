Q: Bhai, pehle yeh contradictory point clear kar lo — "inviscid flow" bolke f=0.03 use karna dono saath nahi ho sakta.

Kya main viscous flow assume karu (f=0.03 ke saath Δp calculate karu), ya strictly inviscid maanu jisme Δp=0 aayega?
A: Haan bhai, sahi pakda tumne — contradiction hai original problem mein.

Tum viscous wala approach follow karo. Matlab:
- Flow viscous treat karo (inviscid wala ignore karo)
- Reynolds number nikalo: Re = ρvD/μ — yeh ke liye oil ki density ~900 kg/m³ aur dynamic viscosity ~0.1 Pa·s (typical lube oil) le sakte ho agar specific value nahi di
- Friction factor Reynolds aur roughness ke hisaab se nikalo — Moody chart ya Colebrook equation use karo
- Phir Δp = f(L/D)(ρv²/2) se pressure drop calculate karo

Yeh approach zyada realistic bhi hai aur textbook standard bhi. Agar problem mein explicitly inviscid bol diya hota toh Δp = 0 hota, lekin f=0.03 diya hai matlab viscous hi maano.

Tum chale jao isse — agar Re aur roughness ke basis pe f ~0.03 ke aaspaas aata hai toh accha, nahi toh exact value se calculate karo.
