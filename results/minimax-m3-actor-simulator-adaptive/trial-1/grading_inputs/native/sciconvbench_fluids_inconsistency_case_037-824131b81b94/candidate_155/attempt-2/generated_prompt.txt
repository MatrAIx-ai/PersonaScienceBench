Compute the pressure drop for steady oil flow in a horizontal pipe using the Darcy–Weisbach equation.
Use the following parameters: pipe length L = 25 m, pipe diameter D = 80 mm (0.08 m), mean velocity V = 1.5 m/s, Darcy friction factor f = 0.03, and oil density ρ = 880 kg/m³.
Treat the problem statement as describing viscous flow and apply the Darcy–Weisbach equation with f = 0.03 as given, ignoring the contradictory "inviscid" wording.
Compute the length-to-diameter ratio L/D = 25 / 0.08 = 312.5.
Compute the dynamic pressure ½ρV² = 0.5 × 880 × (1.5)² = 990 Pa.
Compute the pressure drop ΔP = f · (L/D) · ½ρV² = 0.03 × 312.5 × 990 ≈ 9281 Pa.
Report the final pressure drop as approximately 9.28 kPa (≈ 9.3 kPa).
Note that this result scales linearly with the assumed oil density and that the "inviscid" wording in the original problem is treated as a contradiction and overridden by the explicit f = 0.03 instruction.
