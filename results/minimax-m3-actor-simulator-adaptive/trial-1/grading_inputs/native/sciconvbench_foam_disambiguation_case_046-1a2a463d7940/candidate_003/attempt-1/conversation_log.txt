Q: What is the target Reynolds number ($Re$) for this flow simulation?
A: The full spec doesn't pin a single Re number down explicitly — it just gives the pieces and lets the math do the work:

- Lid velocity U = 1 m/s (from the fixedValue on the movingWall: `(1 0 0)`)
- Cavity length L = 0.1 m
- Kinematic viscosity ν = 0.001 m²/s

Re = U·L / ν = (1 × 0.1) / 0.001 = **100**

So it's a Re ≈ 100 flow. Laminar, steady-state regime. If you wanted something different, you'd have to change U or ν in the case files — the rest is locked in.
