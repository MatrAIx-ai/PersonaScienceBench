Simulate a 2D lid-driven cavity flow with these specifications.
The cavity is a square domain with side length L = 0.1 m, matching the standard lid-driven cavity benchmark geometry.
The top wall moves to the right with uniform velocity U = 1 m/s.
All other walls are stationary and use the no-slip boundary condition.
The fluid is incompressible with kinematic viscosity ν = 0.001 m²/s.
The characteristic velocity is U = 1 m/s and the characteristic length is L = 0.1 m.
The Reynolds number is Re = U·L/ν = 100, indicating laminar flow.
Solve the steady-state incompressible Navier-Stokes equations to obtain the velocity and pressure fields throughout the cavity.
