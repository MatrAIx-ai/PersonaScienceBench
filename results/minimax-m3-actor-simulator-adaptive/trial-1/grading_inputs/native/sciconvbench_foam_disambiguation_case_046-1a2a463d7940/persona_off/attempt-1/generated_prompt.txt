Simulate a 2D incompressible lid-driven cavity flow at Reynolds number 100.

Use a square cavity with characteristic length L = 0.1 m on each side.

Set the lid (top wall) velocity to U = 1 m/s moving in the positive x-direction (rightward), specified as a fixedValue uniform (1 0 0) boundary condition.

Set the bottom, left, and right walls to no-slip zero-velocity boundary conditions (fixedValue uniform (0 0 0)).

Use a fluid with kinematic viscosity ν = 0.001 m²/s, giving Re = U·L/ν = 100.

Assume the flow is laminar and steady-state.

Use an incompressible Navier-Stokes solver (e.g., pimpleFoam, icoFoam, or simpleFoam) with appropriate pressure-velocity coupling.

Use a uniform or graded mesh with sufficient resolution near the walls to resolve boundary layers, with y+ appropriate for a laminar viscous sublayer approach (no wall functions needed).

Continue the simulation until steady-state convergence is achieved based on velocity and pressure residuals.
