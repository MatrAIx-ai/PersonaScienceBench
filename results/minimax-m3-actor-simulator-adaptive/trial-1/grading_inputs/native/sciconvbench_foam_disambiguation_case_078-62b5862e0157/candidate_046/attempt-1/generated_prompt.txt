The simulation uses the interFoam solver from OpenFOAM in 2D mode by declaring the z-direction patches as empty.

The tank geometry is 1.0 m long in x, 0.6 m tall in y, and 0.1 m deep in z.

Generate the mesh with blockMesh using 200 cells in x, 120 cells in y, and 1 cell in z.

Set gravity to (0 -9.81 0) m/s² so the gravity vector points downward in the y-direction.

Run the simulation in transient mode from time 0 to endTime 10 seconds.

Set the initial velocity field U to uniform (0 0 0) so the fluid starts at rest.

Set the initial p_rgh field to uniform 0.

Define the initial alpha.water field as a linear inclined free surface that tilts from 0.25 m depth at x=0 to 0.35 m depth at x=1, assigned through setFieldsDict.

Make the top boundary at y=0.6 m open to atmosphere.

For U at the top boundary use pressureInletOutletVelocity with value uniform (0 0 0).

For p_rgh at the top boundary use totalPressure with p0 uniform 0.

For alpha.water at the top boundary use inletOutlet with inletValue uniform 0.

Declare the side walls at x=0 and x=1 as solid no-slip walls.

For U at the side walls use noSlip.

For alpha.water at the side walls use zeroGradient.

For p_rgh at the side walls use fixedFluxPressure.

Declare the bottom wall at y=0 as a solid no-slip wall with the same boundary conditions as the side walls.

Declare the front and back patches in z as empty to enforce the 2D reduction.

Use adjustable time stepping with an initial deltaT of 1e-3 s.

Set maxCo to 0.5 and maxAlphaCo to 0.5 to keep the MULES limiter stable during the sloshing oscillations.

Configure the simulation output with writeControl adjustableRunTime and writeInterval 0.05 s so that roughly 200 snapshots are written across the 10 s run.

Use binary write format with writePrecision 6 and writeCompression off.

Set the time format to general with timePrecision 6.

Enable runTimeModifiable.

Leave the functions section in controlDict empty for the initial run.

Use standard water and air transport properties, namely nu equal to 1e-6 m²/s and rho equal to 1000 kg/m³ for water and nu equal to 1.5e-5 m²/s and rho equal to 1 kg/m³ for air.

Run laminar with no surface tension set in transportProperties so the sloshing motion is driven purely by the off-equilibrium initial interface and gravity.
