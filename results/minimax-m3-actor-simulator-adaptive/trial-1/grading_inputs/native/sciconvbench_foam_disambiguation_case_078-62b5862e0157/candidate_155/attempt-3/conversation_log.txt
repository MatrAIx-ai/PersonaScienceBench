What simulation duration and time-step strategy would you like?

**Simulation duration:**

**Time-stepping strategy:**
- `deltaT = 1e-3` (initial time step, only used as a starting guess)
- `adjustTimeStep = yes` (adaptive)
- `maxCo = 0.5` (Courant number limit — keeps the interface sharp and stable)

**Rationale:**
With `adjustTimeStep yes` and `maxCo 0.5`, the solver automatically resizes the time step based on the CFL condition, so the nominal `1e-3` is just a fallback. In practice the step will grow/shrink during the run (typically ending up larger once the interface smooths out, or smaller during violent breaking). **Additional entries that should go in `controlDict`:**

```
startFrom       startTime;
startTime       0;
stopAt          endTime;
endTime         10;
deltaT          1e-3;
writeControl    adjustableRunTime;
writeInterval   0.05;
purgeWrite      0;
writeFormat     binary;
writePrecision  6;
writeCompression off;
timeFormat      general;
timePrecision   6;
runTimeModifiable yes;
adjustTimeStep  yes;
maxCo           0.5;
maxAlphaCo      0.5;
maxDeltaT       1;
```

Adding `maxAlphaCo 0.5` next to `maxCo 0.5` is recommended for `interFoam` specifically — it limits the Courant number on the alpha equation so the interface doesn't get smeared at high velocities.
