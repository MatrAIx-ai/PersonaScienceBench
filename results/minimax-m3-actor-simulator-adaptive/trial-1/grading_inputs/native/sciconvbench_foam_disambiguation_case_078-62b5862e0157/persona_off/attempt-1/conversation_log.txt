A few notes and rationale, so you can flag anything you'd want to change:

**Solvers block**

- **`alpha.water.*` (with `nAlphaCorr`, `nAlphaSubCycles`, `cAlpha`):** this is the VOF-specific MULES sub-loop. `nAlphaCorr = 2` is the standard interFoam value — gives the limiter two iterations per PISO step to keep the interface bounded. `nAlphaSubCycles = 1` means no time sub-cycling on alpha (fine for `maxAlphaCo = 0.5` with our cell sizes of 5 mm). `cAlpha = 1` is the interface-compression coefficient used in the `div(phirb,alpha)` term — keep at 1 for sharp interface.

- **`pcorr.*`:** solver for the pressure equation in the *first* PISO corrector, before the actual `p_rgh` solve. PCG + DIC with `tolerance 1e-5` is the standard pairing; tighter is unnecessary since this is just a preconditioner step.

- **`p_rgh` and `p_rghFinal`:** PCG + DIC for both. The first uses `relTol = 0.05` so the inner corrector can converge loosely and let the outer PISO loop tighten things — that's the whole point of PISO iterative-correction. `p_rghFinal` (the last outer corrector) tightens to `relTol = 0` with `tolerance = 1e-7` to get a clean pressure field at the end of the timestep. This is the textbook interFoam pattern.

- **`U` and `UFinal`:** smoothSolver with symGaussSeidel is the standard for velocity in transient VOF — much cheaper than PCG/GAMG per iteration, and `relTol = 0.1` on the inner corrector is plenty (it gets re-smoothed in the next PISO outer step anyway). `UFinal` tightens to relTol = 0.

**Why GAMG is *not* used:** GAMG is faster on large 3D meshes but on a 2D 200×120 = 24,000-cell mesh, PCG/DIC is plenty fast and avoids the GAMG setup overhead per step. For larger 3D cases we'd switch p_rgh to GAMG, but not here.

**PISO block**

- **`nCorrectors = 2`** — two outer PISO corrections per timestep. With two phases and a moving free surface, one corrector is usually not enough to couple pressure and alpha properly; three is overkill with the `relTol` strategy above.
- **`nNonOrthogonalCorrectors = 0`** — the mesh is a regular 200×120 Cartesian block, essentially orthogonal, so no extra non-orthogonal iterations on the pressure Laplacian are needed. I'd bump this to 1 if you start seeing checkerboarding at the walls, but I'd start with 0.
- **`pRefCell = 0`, `pRefValue = 0`** — needed because `p_rgh = p - rho·g·h` is defined only up to a constant; pin the reference at cell 0 with value 0 (gauge). Standard.

**A couple of things I deliberately left out:**

- **No `SIMPLE` block** — interFoam is transient, so PISO is the right algorithm.
- **No `residualControl`** — that's a steady-state SIMPLE thing for convergence on a single timestep. Not needed here.
- **No `potentialFlow` block** — only used at startup for some specific solvers, not interFoam.
- **No `relaxationFactors`** — interFoam doesn't use under-relaxation in the traditional SIMPLE sense; the PISO outer loop + `relTol` does that job.

So in short: **PCG/DIC for pressure, smoothSolver/symGaussSeidel for U and alpha, PISO with nCorrectors = 2 and nNonOrthogonalCorrectors = 0**, fully consistent with the v2312 two-phase `phases (water air)` format and the `maxCo = 0.5` time controls we already picked.
