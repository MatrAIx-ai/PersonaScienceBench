Use the sprayFoam solver, which couples compressible gas-phase transport with Lagrangian spray and PaSR combustion in OpenFOAM.

The geometry is a two-dimensional axisymmetric cylindrical chamber, 0.2 m long along the axial direction with a radius of 0.05 m (diameter 0.1 m), modeled on a wedge with wedge angle 1 deg instead of a full half-domain.

The mesh is generated with blockMesh, using 100 cells along the axial direction and 50 cells in the radial direction, with non-uniform grading that clusters cells toward the axis and toward the inlet face to resolve the spray and the flame root.

Turbulence is modeled with the standard k-epsilon model together with standard wall functions on the side wall, since the mesh is too coarse to resolve the boundary layer.

Gravity is disabled, since this is a compact chamber and buoyancy plays no role on the 50 ms simulation timescale.

The gas phase is compressible with thermophysicalProperties based on the janaf thermo type and the PsiThermo <pureMixture> framework, holding seven species: O2, N2, C12H26 (fuel vapor), CO, CO2, H2, and H2O.

A single liquid phase for n-Dodecane C12H26 is defined in constant/liquidProperties using literature Antoine coefficients for the saturation pressure, a liquid density of 750 kg/m3, and standard liquid thermophysical properties appropriate for nC12H26 near 300 K.

Boundary conditions for the gas phase are as follows: at the inlet the co-flow air is fixed to U = (10 0 0) m/s, T = 800 K, p = 101325 Pa, with composition 0.233 O2 and 0.767 N2, and turbulence specified through 5 percent turbulence intensity which sets k and epsilon from the flow; the dodecane injector occupies the central region of the same inlet face and is defined inside the spray cloud, so the inlet patch itself carries only the gas co-flow values and the axis.

The walls use a noSlip velocity condition, a fixed temperature of 400 K (alpha = 0), zeroGradient pressure, standard wall functions for k, epsilon, and alphat, and zeroGradient for all species; a noFlux condition prevents spray parcels from passing through the wall except through the injector face.

The outlet uses a totalPressure boundary with p0 set to 101325 Pa (so the static pressure relaxes to atmospheric for outflow), inletOutlet velocity with U = 0 if reversed, inletOutlet for k, epsilon, and species, and zeroGradient for T; outlet is non-returning so that spray mass cannot leave the domain through this patch.

The axis is the symmetry boundary (type empty on the wedge, axis type for the fields above in a full axisymmetric setup).

Initial conditions inside the chamber are U = (0 0 0), T = 800 K, p = 101325 Pa, composition 0.233 O2 / 0.767 N2, and turbulence k and epsilon set consistently with 5 percent turbulence intensity and a representative turbulent length scale around 1 percent of the chamber radius.

The spray injector is configured as a coneInjection with coneAngle 15 deg, position at the chamber center on the inlet face, direction along the positive axial direction, massFlowRate 0.002 kg/s (constant in time via the standard injection model), droplet temperature 300 K, injection pressure 101325 Pa, injection duration equal to the full simulation endTime of 0.05 s, and parcelsPerSecond set to 5000.

The droplet size distribution uses RosinRammler with d = 50e-6 m as the representative size, minD = 10e-6 m, maxD = 100e-6 m, and spread parameter n = 3.

Sub-models on the spray cloud use the standard drag model for spherical droplets and the RanzMarshall heat- and mass-transfer correlation (Nu = Sh = 2 + 0.6 Re^0.5 Pr^0.33).

Combustion is handled by the PaSR (Partially Stirred Reactor) model, with PaSRCoeffs set to turbulentReactionTimeCoefficient = 1.0 and Cmix = 1.0, which couples turbulence with finite-rate chemistry.

Gas-phase chemistry uses the standard ODE chemistry solver with two global reversible-but-effectively-irreversible reactions in reactionSchemes.

Reaction 1, the limiting dodecane pyrolysis/partial-oxidation step, is C12H26 + 6 O2 -> 12 CO + 13 H2, declared as Arrhenius with A = 1.0e9 (in consistent units), beta = 0, Ta = 15098 K, and first order with respect to each of C12H26 and O2 in the PaSR context.

Reaction 2, the CO burnout step, is CO + 0.5 O2 -> CO2, declared as Arrhenius with A = 2.2e9, beta = 0, Ta = 15000 K, and first order in CO and O2.

Radiation is not included in this model.

The simulation runs from t = 0 to t = endTime = 0.05 s, with writeControl adjustableRunTime, writeInterval 0.001 s, a maximum Courant number of 0.5 enforced through the PIMPLE outer correctors and maxDeltaT selected automatically, and the run is cold-started (no restart) with ignition occurring auto-thermally from the injection of 300 K dodecane into 800 K air.

Case files to provide are at least constant/transportProperties, constant/thermophysicalProperties, constant/turbulenceProperties, constant/combustionProperties, constant/chemistryProperties, constant/reactions (or equivalent reactionSchemes file), constant/liquidProperties, constant/sprayCloudProperties, constant/g, 0/ directory with U, T, p, species files, k, epsilon, alphat, and the spray positions, and system/blockMeshDict, system/controlDict, system/fvSchemes, system/fvSolution, and system/decomposeParDict (if run in parallel).
