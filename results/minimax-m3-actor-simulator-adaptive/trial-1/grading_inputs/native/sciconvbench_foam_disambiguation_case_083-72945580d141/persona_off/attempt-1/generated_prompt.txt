Use the OpenFOAM solver reactingParcelFoam for a transient simulation of liquid n-dodecane spray combustion in hot co-flow air.
The gas phase uses a pressure-based low-Mach formulation with a standard k-epsilon turbulence model and a diffusionFlame combustion model from constant/combustionProperties.
Set combustionModel to diffusionFlame, active to true, reactionThermo to janaf, and transport to sutherland.
Define a two-step global reaction mechanism in constant/reactionSchemes for n-C12H26 and air.
Step 1 is infinitely fast: C12H26 plus 12.5 O2 yields 12 CO plus 13 H2.
Step 2 is mixing-limited so that turbulence-chemistry interaction is captured through the mixing: CO plus 0.5 O2 yields CO2.
Include gas-phase species O2, N2, CO, CO2, and H2, all using janaf thermodynamics and sutherland transport.
The chamber is a 2D axisymmetric cylinder 0.2 m long in the axial direction and 0.1 m in diameter.
Generate the mesh with blockMesh using 100 cells axial and 50 cells radial, with grading refined near the axis and near the inlet face.
Apply an axis symmetry boundary on the centerline rather than a slip wall.
Inject liquid n-dodecane with a generic Lagrangian cone injection model defined in constant/sprayCloudProperties.
Place the coneInjector at position (0 0 0) at the center of the inlet face, directed axially along the chamber axis with a cone angle of 15 degrees.
Set the spray mass flow rate to 0.002 kg/s and inject 1e5 parcels per second, with each parcel initialized at a droplet temperature of 300 K.
Use a RosinRammler droplet size distribution with a mean diameter of 50e-6 m and a spread parameter of 1.5, accepting defaults for min and max.
Apply the Ranz-Marshall heat and mass transfer model and the standard sphere drag model for the droplets.
The downstream outlet is a fixed-pressure outflow using the totalPressure boundary condition set to 101325 Pa.
Apply zeroGradient at the outlet for velocity, temperature, species mass fractions, k, and epsilon.
The gas-phase inlet supplies co-flow air at velocity U = (10 0 0) m/s, temperature 800 K, and pressure 101325 Pa, with species mass fractions O2 = 0.233 and N2 = 0.767 and no fuel at the inlet.
Set the inlet turbulence quantities with 5 percent turbulence intensity, using k approximately 0.0375 m^2/s^2 and epsilon derived from a characteristic length scale of 0.01 m.
Initialize the chamber interior at t = 0 with stagnant gas (U = 0), temperature 800 K, pressure 101325 Pa, and species mass fractions O2 = 0.233 and N2 = 0.767.
Hold the chamber walls at a fixed temperature of 400 K to model cooled combustion chamber walls.
Run the simulation from 0 to 0.05 s with an adjustableRunTime time-stepping scheme using maxCo = 0.7 and a maxDeltaT cap of 1e-4 s.
Start from an initial deltaT = 1e-6 s.
Write outputs every 0.001 s in binary format using writeControl adjustableRunTime so that writes are evenly spaced in physical time.
Set runTimeModifiable to true in controlDict.
Run the case in a single topology where the gas-phase reacting flow, the diffusion flame chemistry, the k-epsilon turbulence, and the Lagrangian spray cloud are coupled inside reactingParcelFoam using the prescribed BCs, initial conditions, mesh, and time controls.
