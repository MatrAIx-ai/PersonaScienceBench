Simulate a 2D high-speed compressible supersonic jet with inlet Mach number 2.0 discharging into a larger ambient domain.
Treat the working fluid as air modeled as a perfect gas with Sutherland's law for viscosity.
Use a 2D mesh extruded one cell in the z-direction, with `frontAndBack` set to `empty`, located in the x-y plane.
Set the nozzle exit height D to 0.05 m and the overall domain size to 1.0 m (streamwise) by 0.5 m (lateral).
Represent the nozzle exit as a 0.05 m opening at the top of a thin inlet boundary, with a small lip thickness of about 0.01 m at the bottom for the solid wall definition.
Place the far-field boundaries approximately 10 D laterally and 20 D downstream from the nozzle exit within the 1.0 m by 0.5 m domain.
Refine the mesh near the nozzle exit and along the jet centerline to a cell size of approximately D/20, about 0.0025 m, and coarsen toward the far field.
Run the simulation as a viscous laminar flow using `pimpleFoam`.
In `constant/thermophysicalProperties`, configure `type hePsiThermo`, `equationOfState { type perfectGas; }`, `transport sutherland,` and `species { mixture air; }` or equivalent perfect-gas air definition.
In `constant/transportProperties`, set `transportModel sutherland`, dynamic viscosity `mu 1.8e-05`, and Prandtl number `Pr 0.7`.
Set the turbulence model to laminar by using `simulationType laminar` in `constant/turbulenceProperties` (or the equivalent laminar-only turbulence properties entry).
At the nozzle inlet, apply fixed-value boundary conditions with `U (686 0 0)`, `T 300`, and `p 101325` for supersonic inflow.
At the far-field/outlet boundaries, apply `waveTransmissive` for pressure `p` and temperature `T`, and `zeroGradient` for velocity `U`.
At solid walls, apply `noSlip` with `U (0 0 0)` and `zeroGradient` for pressure `p`.
At solid walls, apply a fixed temperature of `T 300` for isothermal walls, or `zeroGradient` for adiabatic walls, with the recommended default being fixed `T 300`.
Initialize the domain with the freestream conditions `U (686 0 0)`, `T 300`, and `p 101325` (or with ambient `U (0 0 0)`, `T 300`, `p 101325`) and let the nozzle inlet drive the jet as the flow establishes.
In `system/controlDict`, set `application pimpleFoam`, choose an end time that allows the jet to establish and the far-field plume to develop (several domain transit times), and use a maximum Courant number of 1 or lower in the PIMPLE algorithm to maintain stability.
Configure `system/fvSchemes` with second-order linearUpwind or limitedLinear for divergence of momentum and energy, appropriate gradient schemes (linear or leastSquares), and standard Laplacian schemes.
Configure `system/fvSolution` for the PIMPLE pressure-velocity coupling with a sufficient number of outer correctors, using a GAMG or PCG solver for pressure with appropriate tolerances and a smooth solver for velocity and energy.
Post-process the results to visualize Mach number contours, pressure fields, temperature fields, and velocity vectors to capture the shock cell structure, jet shear layer, and far-field plume development of the under-expanded Mach 2.0 jet.
