Set up the classic 2D dam-break case in OpenFOAM using the VOF solver interFoam, with a water column collapsing under gravity into air with a sharp free surface.

Use a 2D rectangular domain of length 5 m in x and height 3 m in y, extruded a single cell in z with the frontAndBack patches set to empty.

Divide the x direction into 100 cells and the y direction into 60 cells, producing a uniform cell size of about 0.05 m.

Place the water column adjacent to the left wall, spanning x in [0, 1] m and y in [0, 2] m.

In system/controlDict set the application to interFoam.

Activate gravity with constant value (0 -9.81 0) m/s^2.

Assign the solver startTime to 0 and use a reasonable endTime such as 5 s with a writeInterval of 0.05 s and deltaT around 0.001 s with adjustableRunTime enabled and maxCo below 1.

In the 0 directory create the fields alpha.water, U, and p_rgh.

Initialize alpha.water uniformly to 0 and use the setFieldsDict utility with a box region matching the water column to set alpha.water to 1 inside it and 0 elsewhere.

Initialize U to (0 0 0) everywhere.

Initialize p_rgh to 0 everywhere.

Set alpha.water boundary conditions as follows: inletOutlet with inletValue 1 on the left, right and bottom walls, zeroGradient on the top patch, and empty on the frontAndBack patches.

Set U boundary conditions as follows: noSlip on the left, right and bottom walls, pressureInletOutletVelocity on the top patch, and empty on the frontAndBack patches.

Set p_rgh boundary conditions as follows: fixedFluxPressure on the left, right and bottom walls, totalPressure with p0 equal to atmospheric pressure and U zero on the top patch, and empty on the frontAndBack patches.

In constant/transportProperties define two phases named water and air, assign water density to 1000 kg/m^3 and air density to 1 kg/m^3, assign water kinematic viscosity nu to 1e-6 m^2/s and air kinematic viscosity nu to 1.5e-5 m^2/s, and select the VOF interfacial compression coefficient cAlpha to about 1.

In constant/turbulenceProperties set simulationType to laminar, since the classical dam-break benchmark is usually run without turbulence modeling.

Use the standard solver interFoam to evolve the VOF equation for alpha.water and the shared momentum equation for U coupled with the pressure equation p_rgh.

After running setFields to set alpha.water inside the box, run the interFoam solver from time 0 to the chosen endTime.

Post-process the results to visualize alpha.water isosurfaces and track the water front position for comparison with the classical Koshizuka and Oka or Martin and Moyce experimental benchmark.
