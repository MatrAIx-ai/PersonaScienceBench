Q: Are you sure you want to use `twoPhaseEulerFoam` for this?

The classic dam-break problem is normally solved with `interFoam` (a VOF solver where water and air share a single velocity field and are separated by a sharp captured interface via the `alpha.water` field you described).

`twoPhaseEulerFoam` instead solves an Euler–Euler model in which water and air are two separate interpenetrating continua, each with its own velocity, density, and pressure field, coupled through interfacial drag. Typical use cases are bubble columns, fluidized beds, and sedimentation, not free-surface collapse. For an Euler–Euler dam-break you would usually need fluid-particle drag, particles in the air phase, and the `alpha.air` field treated as the continuous phase rather than just `0`/`1`.
