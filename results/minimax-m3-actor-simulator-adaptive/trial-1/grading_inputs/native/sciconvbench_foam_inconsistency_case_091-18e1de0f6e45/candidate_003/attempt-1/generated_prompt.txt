The simulation is a 2D natural convection benchmark following the classical de Vahl Davis case, solved with the OpenFOAM solver buoyantBoussinesqPimpleFoam.
The flow domain is a square cavity of side length L = 0.0215 m, which together with air properties and ΔT = 10 K yields a target Rayleigh number Ra = 10^4.
The working fluid is air, modeled under the Boussinesq approximation with kinematic viscosity ν ≈ 1.5×10⁻⁵ m²/s and thermal diffusivity α ≈ 2.2×10⁻⁵ m²/s, giving Pr ≈ 0.71.
The thermal expansion coefficient is computed as β = 1/T_ref with reference temperature T_ref = 295 K.
Gravity is specified as g = (0 -9.81 0) m/s² acting in the negative y-direction.
The left wall is a hot isothermal boundary with fixedValue T = 300 K.
The right wall is a cold isothermal boundary with fixedValue T = 290 K.
The top and bottom walls are adiabatic, implemented as zeroGradient for temperature.
All four walls are solid, impermeable, and use noSlip boundary conditions for velocity.
The initial velocity field is zero everywhere.
The initial temperature field is uniform at T = 295 K throughout the cavity.
The simulation is expected to converge to a steady-state single-cell recirculating flow with an average Nusselt number at the hot/cold walls of approximately 2.06, matching the published de Vahl Davis benchmark values for Ra = 10^4.
