Q: You mention that the top and bottom walls are adiabatic, but later specify fixedValue T=295 on those same walls. Which wall condition do you actually want for the top and bottom?
A: Good catch — there's definitely a contradiction there. The top and bottom walls should be **adiabatic**, which in OpenFOAM terms means using the `zeroGradient` boundary condition for temperature on those walls, *not* `fixedValue T=295`.

The `fixedValue T=295` you saw was an error in the requirements. To summarize what I actually want:

- **Left wall (hot):** `fixedValue` T = 300 K
- **Right wall (cold):** `fixedValue` T = 290 K
- **Top and bottom walls (adiabatic):** `zeroGradient` for T
- **All walls:** `noSlip` for velocity

Also, just to be clear on another point: the solver should be **buoyantBoussinesqPimpleFoam**, not laplacianFoam. laplacianFoam only solves a scalar transport equation and doesn't handle the velocity-pressure coupling or buoyancy that we need here.
