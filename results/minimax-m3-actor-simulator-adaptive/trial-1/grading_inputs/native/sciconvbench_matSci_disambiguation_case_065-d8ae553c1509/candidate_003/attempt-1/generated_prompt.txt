The metal has a face-centered cubic (FCC) crystal structure.
The lattice parameter is a = 0.404 nm.
Determine the Burgers vector for a perfect dislocation in an FCC metal, which is the shortest lattice translation vector of the type a/2<110>.
Compute the magnitude of the Burgers vector using the formula b = (a/2) * sqrt(h² + k² + l²) for the <110> direction.
Substitute a = 0.404 nm to obtain b = (0.404/2) * sqrt(2) nm.
Report the final Burgers vector magnitude.
Note that for FCC metals, the perfect dislocation lies on the {111} close-packed plane and slips in the <110> direction.
