Calculate the thermal-mismatch strain of an aluminum film bonded to a ceramic substrate and then cooled.
Use the one-dimensional thermal-mismatch strain formula ε = (α_f − α_s) · ΔT.
Set ΔT = 180 K, the temperature drop imposed during cooling.
Set the substrate CTE to α_s = 8×10⁻⁶ K⁻¹, as provided in the problem statement.
Use the standard room-temperature CTE of aluminum α_f ≈ 23×10⁻⁶ K⁻¹.
Compute the CTE difference Δα = (23 − 8)×10⁻⁶ = 15×10⁻⁶ K⁻¹.
Compute the thermal-mismatch strain ε = 15×10⁻⁶ · 180 = 2.7×10⁻³.
Report the estimated mismatch strain as ε ≈ 2.7×10⁻³, corresponding to roughly 0.27 % tensile in-plane strain in the aluminum film, since aluminum contracts more than the ceramic on cooling and is constrained by the substrate.
Note that this is an order-of-magnitude estimate assuming isotropic linear behavior and uniform temperature change, as is standard for this type of textbook problem.
