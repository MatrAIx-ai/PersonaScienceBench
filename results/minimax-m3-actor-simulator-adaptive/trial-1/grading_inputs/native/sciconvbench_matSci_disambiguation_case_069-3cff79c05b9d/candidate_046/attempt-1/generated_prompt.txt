Treat this as a thermal-mismatch strain estimation for a thin aluminum film bonded to an alumina (Al₂O₃) ceramic substrate.

Use the thermal expansion coefficient of aluminum: α_f = 23×10⁻⁶ K⁻¹.

Use the thermal expansion coefficient of the alumina substrate: α_s = 8×10⁻⁶ K⁻¹.

Apply a temperature change of ΔT = −180 K, corresponding to cooling from the bonding (stress-free) temperature by 180 K.

Assume the substrate is effectively rigid and much thicker than the film, so the substrate imposes its in-plane dimension on the film.

Estimate the free thermal-mismatch (biaxial) strain in the film as ε_mismatch = (α_f − α_s)·|ΔT|.

Compute the differential expansion: Δα = (23 − 8)×10⁻⁶ K⁻¹ = 15×10⁻⁶ K⁻¹.

Compute the strain: ε_mismatch ≈ 15×10⁻⁶ × 180 ≈ 2.7×10⁻³ (≈ 0.27%).

Report the result as a tensile mismatch strain in the aluminum film upon cooling, since α_f > α_s.

State the implicit assumption that elastic constraint and plastic relaxation in the film are neglected in this order-of-magnitude estimate.
