Calculate the electrical resistivity ρ of the wire in Ω·m.
Use the relation ρ = R·A / L, where R is the resistance, A is the cross-section area, and L is the length.
Take R = 11.705 Ω and L = 0.492 m.
Convert the given diameter d = 1.944 mm to meters, so d = 0.001944 m.
Compute the cross-section area using A = π·d² as explicitly specified by the user.
Substitute the numerical values into the resistivity formula: ρ = (11.705 × π × (0.001944)²) / 0.492 Ω·m.
Evaluate the expression to obtain ρ ≈ 2.82 × 10⁻⁴ Ω·m (i.e., about 0.000282 Ω·m).
