Q: To determine the bending stress σ at an arbitrary fiber, I need to know how you want me to handle the bending moment distribution M(x).

Should the bending moment M(x) be left as a general result expressed in terms of an arbitrary load case, or should I commit to a specific load configuration (for example, a simply supported beam under a uniformly distributed load, a center point load, or a cantilever with an end tip load) and derive M(x) explicitly for that case?
A: 不需要推导 M(x),题目给的就是某一个截面处的弯矩值,直接用就行,不必假设载荷形式。

具体参数如下:

- 截面:b = 50 mm,h = 120 mm;中性轴过形心,形心距上下表面各 60 mm;
- 惯性矩 I = b·h³/12 = 50 × 120³ / 12 = **7 200 000 mm⁴**;
- 该截面弯矩 M = **4 000 000 N·mm**(题目写明为 sagging,即下凹为正);
- y 自中性轴起算,**向上为正**。

需要计算应力的三个位置:

1. **y = 0 mm**(中性轴);
2. **顶面纤维**:y = +h/2 = **+60 mm**;
3. **底面纤维**:y = −h/2 = **−60 mm**。

直接用 σ = −M·y / I 这一套符号约定算即可——sagging 弯矩下,正 y 处(顶上)受压为负,负 y 处(底下)受拉为正。

最大应力幅值 |σ_max| = M·c / I = 4 000 000 × 60 / 7 200 000 ≈ **33.33 MPa**。

单位统一用 N–mm–MPa 即可,不需要引入 M(x) 或任何具体载荷工况。
