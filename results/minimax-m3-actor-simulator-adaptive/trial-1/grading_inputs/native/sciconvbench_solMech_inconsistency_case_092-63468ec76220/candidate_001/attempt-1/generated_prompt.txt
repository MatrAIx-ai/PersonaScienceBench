Use N–mm–MPa units and Euler–Bernoulli beam theory (small deflection, linear elastic).

Model the beam as a cantilever of length L = 1800 mm, fixed at x = 0 and free at x = L.

Use the material and section properties of structural steel: Young's modulus E = 200000 MPa and constant second moment of area I = 1200000 mm^4, giving EI = 2.4 × 10¹¹ N·mm².

Apply a downward point load P = 2000 N at the free end x = L.

Use the exact internal bending moment distribution M(x) = P(L − x), not a constant.

Apply the fixed-end boundary conditions v(0) = 0 and v′(0) = 0.

Integrate EI v″(x) = M(x) twice over 0 ≤ x ≤ L, apply the boundary conditions, and evaluate the slope and deflection at x = L.

Compute the free-end slope θ(L) = P L² / (2 EI) and the free-end vertical deflection δ(L) = P L³ / (3 EI).

Report the slope in radians and in degrees and the deflection in mm (downward positive).
