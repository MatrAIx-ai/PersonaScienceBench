Use N–mm–MPa units and Euler–Bernoulli beam theory (small deflection, linear elastic).

Model the cantilever beam as fixed at x = 0 and free at x = L = 1800 mm, with E = 200000 MPa and constant I = 1200000 mm^4.

Apply a downward point load P = 2000 N at the free end x = L.

Use the variable bending moment M(x) = -P(L - x), which gives M(L) = 0 at the free end and M(0) = -PL at the wall.

Apply the boundary conditions at the fixed support: vertical deflection y(0) = 0 and slope θ(0) = 0.

Integrate the elastic line equation EI y''(x) = M(x) = -P(L - x) once to obtain the slope θ(x) = y'(x).

Integrate a second time to obtain the deflection y(x).

Evaluate the slope at x = L and verify it matches θ(L) = PL² / (2EI).

Evaluate the deflection at x = L and verify it matches δ(L) = PL³ / (3EI).

Report the free-end slope in radians (and its equivalent in degrees).

Report the free-end vertical deflection in mm, indicated downward.
