Q: I notice a contradiction in the stated boundary conditions that needs to be resolved before producing a correct specification.

For a cantilever beam (fixed at x=0, free at x=L) with a downward point load P applied at the free end, the standard physics gives:
- Bending moment M(x) = P(L − x), which varies linearly from PL at the fixed end to 0 at the free end (it is NOT constant over the span).
- The free-end rotation is θ(L) = PL²/(2EI), which is NOT zero (the free end slopes downward).
- The free-end deflection is δ(L) = PL³/(3EI).

Your instruction says the free-end rotation is zero and the moment is the constant PL over the full span, which is physically inconsistent with a cantilever carrying a tip point load.

**Which interpretation should I use for the final specification?**

Standard cantilever with tip load (free-end rotation is nonzero, moment varies linearly), or a fixed–guided beam where the moment is constant PL (in which case the deflection shape is different and I need to know the loading that produces this moment)?
A: 您好,感谢您的细致分析。您指出的矛盾确实正确,我需要对此作出澄清。

**完整版任务说明中并未包含那段"自由端转角为零、弯矩取 PL 常数"的特殊约定。** 那段话来自一个错误的、不完整的版本,应以完整的任务说明为准。

请按**标准悬臂梁 + 端部集中载荷**来求解,即:

- 弯矩按实际线性分布:$M(x) = P(L - x)$,从固定端的 $PL$ 线性变化到自由端的 0;
- 不附加任何人为的边界条件约束;
- 使用下列标准结果:

$$\theta(L) = \frac{P L^2}{2 E I}, \qquad \delta(L) = \frac{P L^3}{3 E I}$$

代入给定数值($P = 2000\,\text{N}$, $L = 1800\,\text{mm}$, $E = 200000\,\text{MPa}$, $I = 1\,200\,000\,\text{mm}^4$):

- $E I = 2.4 \times 10^{11}\,\text{N·mm}^2$
- $\theta(L) = \dfrac{2000 \times 1800^2}{2 \times 2.4 \times 10^{11}} = 0.0135\,\text{rad} \approx 0.774^\circ$
- $\delta(L) = \dfrac{2000 \times 1800^3}{3 \times 2.4 \times 10^{11}} = 16.2\,\text{mm}$
