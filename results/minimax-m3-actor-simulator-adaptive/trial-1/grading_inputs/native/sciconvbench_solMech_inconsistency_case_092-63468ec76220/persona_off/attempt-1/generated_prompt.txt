Use SI-consistent units of N, mm, and MPa throughout.
Model the beam with Euler–Bernoulli beam theory assuming small deflections and linear elastic behavior.
Use a cantilever beam total length L = 1800 mm, fixed at x = 0 and free at x = L.
Assume structural steel with Young's modulus E = 200000 MPa.
Assume a constant second moment of area I = 1200000 mm⁴ (so the flexural rigidity is EI = 2.4×10¹¹ N·mm²).
Apply a single downward transverse point load P = 2000 N at the free end x = L.
Take the bending moment as M(x) = P·(L − x), linearly varying from −P·L at the fixed end to 0 at the free end.
Apply the fixed-end boundary conditions v(0) = 0 and v′(0) = 0.
Integrate EI·v″(x) = M(x) twice and apply the two boundary conditions to determine the integration constants.
Report the free-end slope θ(L) using the closed-form result θ(L) = P·L² / (2·EI).
Report the free-end deflection v(L) using the closed-form result v(L) = P·L³ / (3·EI).
Sign both quantities as negative to indicate downward deflection and clockwise rotation of the free end.
Compute and report the free-end slope in radians and additionally convert and report it in degrees.
Report the free-end vertical deflection in mm.
