Solve a 2D small-strain linear elasticity problem in plane stress using legacy FEniCS (dolfin) with a vector-valued displacement space and a direct linear solver.
Use material parameters E=70e9 Pa, ν=0.33, ρ=2700 kg/m^3, and g=9.81 m/s^2.
Assume unit thickness and apply the reduced plane-stress constitutive law.
Define the domain as a parallelogram with vertices (0,0), (1,0), (1.2,0.3), and (0.2,0.3).
Discretize the domain with 72 mesh elements.
Apply gravity as a body force of magnitude ρ·g acting in the -y direction.
Apply a horizontal point load of -5000 N in the -x direction at the point (1.1, 0.15), which is the midpoint of the right inclined edge connecting (1,0) and (1.2,0.3), using a PointSource on the x-component of the displacement vector (DOF index 0) with the negative value.
Clamp the left inclined edge, corresponding to the boundary segment from (0,0) to (0.2,0.3) given by y=1.5x, by imposing DirichletBC(V, Constant((0,0)), ...) on it.
Solve the resulting variational problem using a direct linear solver.
Compute the displacement field and the in-plane Cauchy stress field.
Compute the von Mises stress distribution from the in-plane Cauchy stress field over the non-rectangular geometry.
