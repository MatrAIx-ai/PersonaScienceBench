Q: Which inclined edge is clamped: the left edge from (0.2, 0.3) to (0, 0) or the right edge from (1, 0) to (1.2, 0.3)?
A: The left inclined edge — the one from (0,0) up to (0.2, 0.3), which is exactly the slope y = 1.5x — is clamped (u = 0 there, both components).

The right slanted edge from (1,0) to (1.2, 0.3) is free, and that's where the −5 kN horizontal point load goes, at the boundary point (1.1, 0.15).
