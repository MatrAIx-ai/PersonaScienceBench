Solve a plane-stress linear elasticity problem on a parallelogram domain with FEniCS legacy (dolfin) using a vector-valued displacement space and a direct linear solver.
Use E = 70e9 Pa, Poisson ratio nu = 0.33, density rho = 2700 kg/m^3, and gravity g = 9.81 m/s^2, assuming a unit out-of-plane thickness.
Define the domain as a parallelogram with vertices (0,0), (1,0), (1.2,0.3), (0.2,0.3), oriented so that the bottom edge y=0 runs from (0,0) to (1,0) and the top edge y=0.3 runs from (0.2,0.3) to (1.2,0.3).
Generate a triangular mesh of the parallelogram consisting of exactly 72 cells.
Impose a Dirichlet boundary condition u = 0 on the left inclined edge defined by the segment from (0,0) to (0.2,0.3) (the line y = 1.5 x).
Treat the remaining three edges as traction-free except where the point load is applied.
Apply a body force b = (0, - rho * g) representing gravity.
Apply a horizontal point load of P = -5000 N (i.e., 5 kN in the negative x direction) at the point (1.1, 0.15) on the right inclined edge, which lies exactly on the segment from (1,0) to (1.2,0.3).
Use the reduced plane-stress constitutive law relating the in-plane Cauchy stress sigma to the in-plane strain epsilon, using E and nu as given.
Formulate the standard linear equilibrium variational problem with the displacement test and trial space being vector-valued continuous Lagrange elements of degree 1.
Implement the point load as a concentrated force contribution to the linear form, for example using a PointSource attached to the x-component of the load vector at the DOF nearest to (1.1, 0.15), or by adding P * v_x evaluated at that point to the right-hand side.
Use a direct linear solver (LU) from the legacy FEniCS linear algebra back end to solve the resulting symmetric positive-definite system.
Project the displacement field for visualization as a vector field and as magnitude contours.
Recover the in-plane Cauchy stress field from the displacement by applying the plane-stress constitutive law pointwise.
Compute the von Mises stress under plane stress using sigma_vm = sqrt(sigma_xx^2 + sigma_yy^2 - sigma_xx*sigma_yy + 3*sigma_xy^2).
Plot the deformed configuration (scaled) overlaid on the von Mises stress field, and report the maximum displacement magnitude and the maximum von Mises stress.
Deliverable is a runnable legacy FEniCS script and the resulting displacement and von Mises stress plots.
