Q: Where is the horizontal point load applied and what is its magnitude?
A: The horizontal point load is **-5 kN** (i.e., 5000 N in the negative x-direction, pointing to the left) applied at the point **(1.1, 0.15)**. This point lies on the right slanted edge of the parallelogram, somewhere between the vertices (1, 0) and (1.2, 0.3).

As noted in the implementation notes, please apply it as a `PointSource` on the x-component of the displacement space (rather than smearing it over an edge), with a value of `-5000.0` N. The left inclined edge (along y = 1.5x, from (0,0) to (0.2, 0.3)) remains clamped.
